    How to play

    • Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, paper beats rock.
    • First to win three rounds takes the match. Draws don't count.
    • The computer picks at random with the browser's secure random generator.

    Keyboard: R rock, P paper, S scissors, N new match.
