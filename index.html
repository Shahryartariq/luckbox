luckbox

Six quick games of luck and nerve. Pick a ticket and play. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for, and your records stay in this browser.

  • Blackjack Beat the dealer to 21. Hit, stand or double down, with chips that carry over between visits. Not played yet
  • Mines Flip tiles on a 5 by 5 grid. Every safe tile raises your multiplier. Cash out before you hit a mine. Not played yet
  • Higher or Lower Guess if the next card beats this one. The odds are shown, so the only question is how brave you feel. Not played yet
  • Pig Roll the die and bank your points before a 1 wipes them out. First to 50 beats the computer. Not played yet
  • Rock Paper Scissors The classic, played first to three against a computer that picks at random. No tricks. Not played yet
  • Number Guess Find the secret number with as few guesses as you can. Each miss narrows the range. Not played yet
