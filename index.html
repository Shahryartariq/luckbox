luckbox

Eight quick games, from pure luck to pure logic. Pick a ticket and play. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for, and your records stay in this browser.

  • Blackjack Beat the dealer to 21. Hit, stand, double down or split, with chips that carry over between visits. Not played yet
  • Mines Flip tiles on a 5 by 5 grid. Every safe tile raises your multiplier. Cash out before you hit a mine. Not played yet
  • Higher or Lower Guess if the next card beats this one. The odds are shown, so the only question is how brave you feel. Not played yet
  • Sudoku The one game here where luck can't help you. Fresh puzzles every time, each with exactly one solution. Not played yet
  • 2048 Slide the tiles and merge equal numbers. New tiles drop in at random, so plan ahead and reach 2048. Not played yet
  • Tic Tac Toe The classic three in a row. Play a friend, or the computer on easy, medium or unbeatable. Can you hold it to a draw? Not played yet
  • Rock Paper Scissors The classic, played first to three against a computer that picks at random. No tricks. Not played yet
  • Number Guess Find the secret number with as few guesses as you can. Each miss narrows the range. Not played yet
