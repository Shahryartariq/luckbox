How to play

  • Roll as often as you like. Each roll adds to this turn's points.
  • Roll a 1 and you lose this turn's points and the turn passes.
  • Bank to keep your points safe. First to 50 wins.
  • The computer banks around 20 points a turn and plays harder when you are close to winning.

Keyboard: R roll, B bank, N new game.
