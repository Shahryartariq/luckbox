How to play

  • A card is face up. Call whether the next one is higher or lower. Aces are high.
  • Every right call adds one to your streak. Same rank is a tie and your run goes on.
  • The odds come from the cards still in the deck, so they change as cards are used.
  • One wrong call ends the run.

Keyboard: Up arrow for higher, down arrow for lower, N new run.
