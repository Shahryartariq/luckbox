How to play

  • Take turns placing X and O. Three in a row, column or diagonal wins.
  • You are X. Against the computer, who goes first switches every game.
  • Easy plays at random, medium blocks your wins most of the time, and unbeatable plays perfectly: the best you can do is a draw.
  • Two players share one screen. Your game is saved if you leave.

Keyboard: 1 to 9 place a mark (top left to bottom right), arrow keys move, N for a new game.
