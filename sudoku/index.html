How to play

  • Fill the board so every row, column and 3 by 3 box has the numbers 1 to 9 once.
  • Pick a cell, then a number. Numbers that clash turn red, and the pad shows how many of each are left.
  • Turn on Notes to pencil in candidates. Placing a number clears it from the notes around it.
  • Hints fill in one cell. Best times only count puzzles solved without hints. Your puzzle is saved if you leave.

Keyboard: arrow keys move, 1 to 9 place a number, Backspace erases, N toggles notes, U or Ctrl+Z undoes, H gives a hint.
