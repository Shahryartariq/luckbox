How to play

  • Pick a bet and deal. Cards are worth their number, faces are 10 and aces are 1 or 11.
  • Hit to take a card, stand to keep your total. Double doubles your bet for exactly one more card.
  • The dealer draws to 17 and stands on all 17s. Blackjack pays 3 to 2.
  • Four decks are shuffled together and reshuffled when the shoe runs low. Your chips are saved in this browser.

Keyboard: N deal, H hit, S stand, D double.
