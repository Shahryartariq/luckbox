How to play

  • Slide all the tiles up, down, left or right. Two tiles with the same number merge into one.
  • After every move a new 2 or 4 appears in a random empty cell.
  • Reach the 2048 tile to win, then keep going for a bigger score if you like.
  • The game ends when the board is full and no tiles can merge. Your game is saved if you leave.

Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to slide, N for a new game. On a phone, swipe on the board.
