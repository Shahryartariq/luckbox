How to play

  • Choose how many mines hide in the 25 tiles. More mines, faster multiplier.
  • Each round costs 10 points. Flip tiles one at a time.
  • Cash out whenever you like to take 10 points times your multiplier. Hit a mine and the round is lost.
  • Multipliers are fair: the odds of surviving times the payout always equals your stake.

Keyboard: C cash out, N new round.
